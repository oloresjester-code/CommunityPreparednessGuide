Emergency kit basics

💧

Water and food

Store drinking water and food that can be kept safely.

🔦

Light and power

Keep a flashlight, spare batteries, and a charged power bank if available.

🩹

First aid

Include basic first-aid supplies and any essential personal items.

📄

Important documents

Protect copies of important documents and emergency contact details.

FEATURE 3

General evacuation reminders

  • Know the evacuation routes and designated evacuation centers announced by your local authorities.
  • Keep your phone charged when possible and make a family communication plan.
  • Bring essential supplies if you are instructed to evacuate.
  • Follow official instructions and do not rely on this educational page for real-time emergency information.
